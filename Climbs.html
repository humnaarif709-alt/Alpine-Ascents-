Mount Everest Detailed Guide
Route Overview & Acclimatization

Mount Everest requires approximately 6 to 8 weeks for acclimatization, rotative climbs to higher camps, and waiting for favorable weather windows in May. Key technical sections include crossing the dangerous Khumbu Icefall, Lhotse Face, and Hillary Step.

  • Expedition Duration: 60 Days
  • Difficulty Grade: Extreme High-Altitude
  • Optimal Season: April - May

K2 Detailed Guide
Technical Challenge & Strategy

K2 presents technical rock and ice climbing with near-vertical ascents along the Abruzzi Spur. Climbers face the Bottleneck couloir under treacherous ice seracs above 8,000m altitude.

  • Expedition Duration: 50 Days
  • Difficulty Grade: Expert / Highly Technical
  • Optimal Season: June - August
Book Expedition
Mont Blanc Detailed Guide
Classic Alpine Route

The Goûter Route provides standard access to the summit. Climbers traverse snowy ridges, cross crevasse fields, and scramble past the Goûter hut in early morning hours to summit before afternoon temperatures rise.

  • Expedition Duration: 6 Days
  • Difficulty Grade: Intermediate Alpine
  • Optimal Season: June - September
