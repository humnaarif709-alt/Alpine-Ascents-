Overview

Circle Europe's most famous mountain massif while enjoying scenic mountain passes, alpine huts, and local cuisine. The Tour du Mont Blanc passes through France, Italy, and Switzerland - 170km of pure Alpine magic.

What You Will Experience:

  • ✓ Complete Circuit of Mont Blanc Massif (170km)
  • ✓ 3 Countries in 1 Trek - France, Italy, Switzerland
  • ✓ Stay in Authentic Alpine Huts & Mountain Refuges
  • ✓ Scenic Passes: Grand Col Ferret, Seigne, Bonhomme
  • ✓ Local Alpine Cuisine & Culture
  • ✓ No Camping - Comfortable Hut to Hut Trek

Day Wise Schedule:

Day 1-3: Chamonix to Les Chapieux via Les Contamines
Day 4-6: Italy Section - Courmayeur & Val Ferret
Day 7-10: Switzerland Section - Champex to Chamonix, Final Celebration

Included:

All hut accommodations, breakfast & dinner, professional mountain guide, luggage transfer, and TMB permit.
