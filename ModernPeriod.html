3. Modern Period - 1950 to Present

The Modern Period started after World War 2. The biggest goal was to climb all 14 mountains above 8000m called "Eight-Thousanders". Technology like weather forecasting, synthetic clothes, and oxygen made climbing safer. This era is about speed, solo climbs, and also protecting the environment.

Why Did People Climb?

  • Records: To climb all 8000m peaks and set speed records
  • Adventure: New styles like Alpine Style - light, fast, no oxygen
  • Science & Media: Documentaries, research, and sponsorships
  • Tourism: Commercial expeditions for normal people to climb Everest

Key Events

  • 1950: First 8000m peak climbed - Annapurna by French team
  • 1953: First ascent of Mount Everest by Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay
  • 1986: Reinhold Messner climbed all 14 Eight-Thousanders without oxygen
  • 2019: Nirmal Purja climbed all 14 Eight-Thousanders in just 6 months - World Record

Equipment in Modern Period

Lightweight synthetic jackets, plastic boots, oxygen cylinders, GPS, satellite phones, and strong nylon ropes are used now. Focus is also on "Leave No Trace" to keep mountains clean and safe.
