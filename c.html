Why Do We Climb Mountains?

Kyuki pahar hame bulate hain. Neeche ki duniya shor se bhari hai, upar ja kar hi sukoon milta hai. Jab aap top par khade hote ho to lagta hai aapne duniya jeet li.

Hum is liye chadhte hain taake apne dar ko hara sakein, apni limits ko tod sakein. Har qadam ek nayi kahani hai.

Latest Thought:

Climbing is not about reaching the top, it's about the journey.
