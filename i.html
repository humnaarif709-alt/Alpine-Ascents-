Course Overview

This course is for climbers who have completed the beginner course and want to go to the next level. In this course, we will prepare you for 4000m - 5000m peaks. Full focus will be on safety, speed, and technical skills.

What You Will Learn:

  • ✓ Advanced Rope Work & Anchor Building
  • ✓ Steep Ice Climbing (60-70 Degree)
  • ✓ Crevasse Rescue - Real Scenario Training
  • ✓ Avalanche Awareness & Safety
  • ✓ Navigation & Weather Reading
  • ✓ 1 Night Glacier Camping

Day Wise Schedule:

Day 1-2: Revision + Advanced knots & rescue
Day 3-4: Ice wall climbing & glacier travel
Day 5: Final Exam Climb + Certification

Requirement:

A beginner course certificate or 2+ mountain experiences are required.
