OUR STANDARDS

COMPANY PRINCIPLES

01

EXPERIENCED GUIDES

We value knowledgeable guides who can teach, communicate effectively and make responsible decisions in changing mountain conditions.

02

RISK AWARENESS

Mountain environments can change quickly. Our guiding approach emphasizes preparation, observation, communication and responsible decision-making.

03
