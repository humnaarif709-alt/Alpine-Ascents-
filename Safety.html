OUR STANDARDS

EXPEDITION SAFETY PROTOCOLS

Certified Guides

Our team consists of expert guides with decades of mountain navigation experience and wild country credentials.

  • Wilderness First Responder (WFR) certified
  • IFMGA / AMGA certified lead guides
  • Comprehensive high-altitude training

Satellite Communication

Every expedition stays connected with base camp and medical support using top-grade emergency communication tech.

  • Garmin InReach satellite tracking
  • 24/7 direct link to medical support
  • Real-time weather forecast tracking

Medical Support

We pack comprehensive medical kits and supplemental oxygen systems tailored for extreme alpine environments.

  • Pulse oximeters for daily health checks
  • Gamow hyperbaric bags on high peaks
  • Emergency evacuation protocols

Weather Monitoring

We work with specialized mountain meteorologists to analyze forecasts before committing to summit pushes.

  • Daily wind speed & temperature monitoring
  • Avalanche risk assessments
  • Flexible contingency days

Acclimatization Planning

Schedules are built to allow your body to adapt safely to rising elevation levels without rushing.

  • "Climb high, sleep low" strategy
  • Monitored hydration & oxygenation intake
  • Paced rest days at designated camps

Gear Checks

Ropes, harnesses, crampons, and hardware undergo multi-point safety inspections prior to every climb.

  • UIAA-certified ropes & hardware
  • Pre-expedition client gear checks
  • Redundant anchor & safety rigging
