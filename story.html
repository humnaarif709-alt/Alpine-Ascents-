Founded in 1986, Alpine Ascents International has been a leader in mountaineering for over 38 years. We started with a simple mission: to provide world-class guiding with safety and education as our core values.

38+Years of Experience

Our Mission

We believe that preparation, teamwork, responsible climbing and professional instruction are essential parts of a successful adventure. Our mountain guides bring years of experience to every expedition.

Our Values

Safety is always first. We promote Leave No Trace, respect local cultures, and train the next generation of climbers to climb responsibly and sustainably.

Why Choose Us?

  • Certified IFMGA / AMGA Guides
  • Small group size for personal attention
  • Best safety record in the industry
  • Environmentally responsible expeditions
