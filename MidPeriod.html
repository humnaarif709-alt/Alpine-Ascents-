2. Mid Period - 1850 to 1950

The 19th and early 20th century is called the "Golden Age of Mountaineering". This was when climbing became a proper sport. Rich people from Europe, especially Britain, started coming to the Alps with professional guides. The main goal was to climb every big peak in the Alps. New equipment like ropes, ice axes, and crampons were invented.

Why Did People Climb?

  • Sport: Climbing became a hobby and competition for adventure and fame
  • Science: Scientists climbed to study weather, rocks, and plants at high altitude
  • National Pride: Countries competed to have their people climb the first ascents
  • Tourism: Mountain hotels and railways were built to bring tourists

Key Events

  • 1857: Alpine Club founded in London - First mountaineering club in the world
  • 1865: First ascent of Matterhorn by Edward Whymper. This was the most famous climb of the era
  • 1895: First winter ascent of Mont Blanc
  • 1924: George Mallory and Andrew Irvine disappeared on Mount Everest

Equipment in Mid Period

Better boots with nails, hemp ropes, wooden ice axes, and wool clothes were used. Pitons and carabiners were invented for safety. Tents and stoves made it possible to stay on mountains for many days.
