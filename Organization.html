Mountaineering Organizations & Clubs

Connecting global climbers, managing high-altitude expeditions, and building mountain safety networks.

International & National Organizations

Alpine Club of Pakistan (ACP)

Islamabad, Pakistan

The official national mountaineering federation regulating climbing activities, permits, and training across Karakoram and Himalayas.

Website: alpineclub.org.pk

Contact: +92 51 9215282 | info@alpineclub.org.pk

Location: Jinnah Sports Complex, Islamabad

UIAA - World Climbing Body

Bern, Switzerland

International Climbing and Mountaineering Federation setting global equipment safety standards, competition rules, and ethics.

Website: theuiaa.org

Contact: info@theuiaa.org

Location: Monbijoustrasse 61, Bern

The Alpine Club (UK)

London, United Kingdom

Founded in 1857, it is the world's oldest mountaineering club, archiving historic Himalayan and Alpine exploration records.

Website: alpine-club.org.uk

Contact: admin@alpine-club.org.uk

Location: 55 Charlotte Rd, London

Prominent Regional Clubs & Expeditions

Alpine Club of Pakistan (National HQ)

  • Location: Jinnah Sports Complex, Islamabad, Pakistan
  • Activities: High-altitude expedition management, rock climbing championships, ice climbing, and glacier navigation across Karakoram and Himalayas.
  • Training: Comprehensive mountaineering courses, avalanche rescue drills, and wilderness safety certifications.
  • Expeditions: Organizes national & international ascents to K2, Nanga Parbat, Broad Peak, and Spantik.
  • Contact: info@alpineclub.org.pk | +92 51 9215282

Karimabad Climbing & Trekking Club

  • Location: Karimabad, Hunza Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan
  • Activities: Granite wall rock climbing, high-altitude guiding, bouldering, and winter ice climbing near Passu and Ultar Sar.
  • Training: High Altitude Porter (HAP) professional safety courses, eco-friendly climbing techniques, and crevasse rescue operations.
  • Expeditions: Guided treks to Rakaposhi Base Camp, Ladyfinger Peak, and Rush Lake.
  • Contact: hunzaclimbing@gmail.com | +92 355 4123456

Baltistan Alpine Rescue & Climbing Club

  • Location: Skardu City, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan
  • Activities: Technical big-wall rock climbing, glacier logistics management, and emergency high-altitude rescue operations.
  • Training: Specialized winter survival techniques, fixed-rope management, and high-altitude emergency first aid.
  • Expeditions: Trango Towers technical ascents, K2 Basecamp logistics, and Concordia treks.
  • Contact: baltistanclimbing@gmail.com | +92 358 1122334

Mountain Adventure Club

  • Location: Chamonix-Mont-Blanc, France
  • Activities: Alpine rock climbing, multi-pitch ascents, high-altitude ski touring, and technical bouldering.
  • Training: Certified IFMGA guide workshops, wilderness survival, rope handling, and ice axe navigation skills.
  • Expeditions: Mont Blanc ascents, Matterhorn ridge climbs, and Himalayan trekking routes.
  • Contact: contact@mountainclub.org | +33 4 50 53 00 00
