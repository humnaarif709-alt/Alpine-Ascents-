History of Mountaineering

Journey through time to discover how human bravery and passion transformed dangerous peaks into world-renowned expedition adventures!

Early Period

Before the 18th century, people climbed mountains mainly for religious reasons, hunting, or to find new routes. Mountains were considered dangerous and sacred places. There was no sport involved, only survival and faith.

Key Points:

1336
First recreational climb - Mount Ventoux
1786
First ascent of Mont Blanc
Read More

Mid Period / Golden Age

The 19th century is called the "Golden Age of Mountaineering". Rich Europeans started climbing with professional guides. Better equipment like ropes and ice axes were invented.

Key Points:

1857
Alpine Club founded in London
1865
First ascent of the Matterhorn
1870s
Climbing spread to Himalayas and Andes
Read More

Modern Period

The modern period started after World War 2. The goal was to climb all 14 peaks above 8000m. Technology and synthetic clothing made climbing safer.

Key Points:

1953
First ascent of Mount Everest
1986
Reinhold Messner all 14 Eight-Thousands
2019
Nirmal Purja set world record
